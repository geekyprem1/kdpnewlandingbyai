A QUICK HELPING HAND

Let's clear
the small stuff.

A few answers to help you get back to your books.

Which email should I use to log in?

Use the email address you provided when purchasing BookNexa. Check your purchase receipt if you're unsure which address you used.

My password isn't working. What now?

Check for extra spaces when copying the starter password. If you changed your password, use your updated one. Still stuck? Email support with your purchase email and order ID.

I can't find my purchase confirmation.

Check your spam, junk, and promotions folders, then search your inbox for your order receipt. If you still can't find it, contact support with the email you used to purchase.

What should I include in a support request?

Include your purchase email, order ID, a short description of the issue, and any relevant screenshots or error messages. Never send your password.

When will I hear back from support?

Our team replies within 24–48 hours, excluding Sundays. Requests are handled in the order they arrive.
